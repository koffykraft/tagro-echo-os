# TAGRO ECHO OS Observer Contract

Status: Founding contract
Authority: Constitution + DEC-0004

## Purpose
The Observer is the rear-seat system. It watches the operating system, components, relationships and composite outcomes without becoming an uncontrolled second driver.

## Allowed actions
The Observer may:
- read admitted events and permitted evidence;
- compare component outputs;
- detect mismatch, drift, latency, staleness and unusual patterns;
- calculate derived observations;
- create findings, questions and attention items;
- learn from confirmed outcomes;
- report confidence, assumptions and rule/model version;
- measure its own false-alert and miss rates.

## Forbidden actions
The Observer may not directly:
- create or confirm a sale;
- adjust stock;
- receive or allocate payment;
- close cash;
- approve service work;
- create a binding purchase order;
- post to BUSY;
- alter customer/machine ownership;
- send consequential external communications;
- mutate operational truth.

If an automated action is later approved, it becomes a separately contracted Driver capability with explicit authority. Observer powers do not expand silently.

## Finding contract
Every material finding must retain:
- finding ID;
- time;
- evidence/event references;
- components compared;
- rule/model/version;
- assumptions;
- confidence;
- recommended review/action;
- later disposition when known.

## Composite honesty
The Observer must inspect not only whether components are internally correct, but whether relationships between their outputs create a faithful composite business conclusion.

Example: a correct bank credit plus a correct outstanding invoice does not prove that the credit paid that invoice. The relationship requires evidence.

## Self-observation
Observer quality is itself observable. The system must preserve enough information to identify rules/models that become noisy, biased, stale or misleading and replace them without changing operational history.
