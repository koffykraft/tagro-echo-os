# TAGRO ECHO OS Constitution

Version: 1.1
Status: Owner-approved governing law
Effective: 2026-08-22
Supersedes: Version 1.0 dated 2026-08-19

## 1. Purpose
TAGRO ECHO OS is an independent, AWS-first, mobile-first operating system for the ECHO venture. It is designed for a distributed network projected toward approximately 100 counters across seven districts, with phone operation primary and PC/laptop optional.

This Constitution governs every implementation, AI builder, human developer, integration, deployment, replacement and user-facing operating experience.

Version 1.1 explicitly recognizes that the first implementation stage established foundations and evidence, not a permanent ceiling on architecture, user experience, tools, environments or methods.

## 2. Supremacy
The active system must obey, in order:
1. Reality and lawful owner authority.
2. This Constitution.
3. Versioned core contracts and schemas.
4. Owner-approved architecture and structural-design decisions.
5. Component contracts.
6. Acceptance criteria.
7. Current state.
8. The active work order.

No chat, handoff, code comment, previous TAGRO implementation, AI suggestion, deployment convenience, sunk cost, vendor limitation or earlier-stage design choice overrides this hierarchy.

## 3. Independence
TAGRO ECHO OS is not a fork, skin, module or runtime dependency of the older TAGRO OS, STIHL or Jain systems.
Useful historical implementations may be studied as evidence. Nothing enters TAGRO ECHO OS automatically. Every legacy idea or component must independently pass admission.

The same principle applies internally: an earlier ECHO implementation does not become permanent merely because it exists, works, has tests, has already been deployed in NonProd or was once the best available option.

## 4. Enduring skeleton
The enduring skeleton contains:
- Identity
- Event
- Evidence
- Relationship
- Time
- Location
- Authority
- State
- Provenance
- Confidence

Business domains, user experiences, AI models, databases, cloud providers, accounting packages and adapters are replaceable structures around the skeleton.

Even the skeleton is versioned and replaceable through explicit migration. No migration may silently reinterpret old facts.

## 5. Governed structural layers
ECHO is not only a backend foundation with screens added later. The operating system is formed by coordinated structural layers around the enduring skeleton.

At minimum these include:
- operational command / Driver structure;
- Observer / attention / intelligence structure;
- data, persistence and event structure;
- evidence and provenance structure;
- integration / dock structure;
- user-experience and interaction structure;
- device/environment structure;
- recovery, continuity and observability structure.

The user-experience structure is therefore governed architecture. It determines how authorised people perceive context, perform jobs, receive feedback, recover interrupted work, understand state and reach consequential actions. It may require changes to workflow, shell, navigation, client architecture, input methods, projections and integration boundaries when evidence shows those changes are needed.

UI/UX is not subordinate cosmetic treatment and must not be postponed until after technical implementation if doing so would lock in a poorer operating model.

## 6. Reality first
The deepest unit of the OS is a real event involving real entities, not a page, report, table, status dropdown or message.
One event may project into many authorised views. Those projections must not become independent copies of truth.
State is derived from admitted events wherever practical. Corrections create new evidence/events and supersession links rather than silently rewriting history.

The interface must simplify access to reality without replacing reality with screen state.

## 7. Evidence and interpretation
Evidence is preserved separately from interpretation.
A photo is evidence. An AI recognition is an observation. A confirmed stock count is an admitted business event.
Raw evidence must not be overwritten by canonical names, AI conclusions or later corrections.
Every consequential derived fact must retain enough provenance to explain how it was produced.

## 8. Honesty
Honesty is required at three levels:
- Component honesty: a component accurately represents what it observed, received and produced.
- Relationship honesty: connections between otherwise correct facts are themselves evidenced and appropriately confident.
- Composite honesty: the combined business conclusion must accurately represent reality.

Unknown remains unknown. Stale remains stale. Inferred remains inferred. Missing does not become zero. Accepted does not mean completed. AI confidence does not equal confirmation.

The user experience must preserve the same honesty: visually simple must never mean semantically misleading.

## 9. Front-seat Driver
The operational system drives the business.
It may perform authorised commands such as sale, stock movement, service intake, payment, delivery, purchase, cash close and accounting handoff.
Consequential writes require deterministic validation and the required human/system authority.
The operational path must continue safely when optional intelligence is unavailable.

## 10. Rear-seat Observer
The Observer is structurally read-only with respect to operational command.
It may watch, compare, detect, calculate, question, report, learn from confirmed outcomes and create attention/review recommendations.
It may not silently sell, adjust stock, receive payment, close cash, approve service, order inventory, post to BUSY, send consequential external communications, alter identity/ownership or change operational truth.
The Observer must also observe its own accuracy, drift, false alerts, assumptions and model/rule versions.

## 11. AI authority
AI may observe, extract, recognise, associate, classify, calculate, retrieve, explain, propose, draft and prepare.
AI alone may not establish consequential business truth.
AI-proposed observations require the confirmation or deterministic rule stated in the owning component contract before they create an admitted event.
AI builders have no architectural sovereignty. They operate within active owner authority, governance and work orders.

AI should preferentially reduce searching, typing, remembering and reconciliation burden rather than add unnecessary interface complexity.

## 12. Replaceability
Every component must declare purpose, inputs, outputs, state/truth owned, authority, dependencies, events consumed/emitted, failure behaviour, security boundary, observer visibility, replacement/migration contract and acceptance tests.
A component that cannot be removed or replaced without unknowable consequences is not admitted until that dependency is understood and governed.
Cloud providers and vendors are adapters/engines, not the skeleton.

Replacement includes user-interface frameworks, hosting environments, databases, AI providers, logistics providers, payment providers, accounting engines, design systems, mobile/web/native approaches, build tools and other technical or operational structures.

## 13. Evolution duty — better solutions must be considered
ECHO must not preserve a tool, design, environment, provider, architecture or workflow solely because it was selected earlier, has accumulated work, or is already familiar to builders.

When credible evidence shows that an existing choice has persistent flaws, creates recurring friction, carries unacceptable risk, is materially inadequate for TAGRO's future scale or operating model, or when a materially better solution becomes available, the system has a duty to consider the alternative.

Consideration does not mean automatic replacement. It requires due diligence and a comprehensive Planar and Prismatic review before admission.

The review must examine, as applicable:
- the real problem and evidence of inadequacy;
- current and future users and operating environments;
- identity, event, evidence, relationship, time, location, authority, state, provenance and confidence consequences;
- Driver and Observer consequences;
- all affected business domains and receivers;
- upstream and downstream systems;
- security, privacy, resilience, performance and cost;
- mobile, desktop, offline and field-operation consequences;
- migration, coexistence, rollback and recovery;
- vendor lock-in and replaceability;
- data/evidence preservation and semantic compatibility;
- usability, accessibility, comfort and adoption;
- future scale and foreseeable TAGRO operating models;
- failure modes and blast radius;
- alternatives considered and why they were rejected or preferred.

The Planar review examines the candidate across all relevant dimensions and receivers rather than from one page, team or subsystem.

The Prismatic review deliberately steps outward when a narrow framing is insufficient: it compares broader interpretations, environments, tools and solution classes before converging on a choice. A current implementation is one ray/evidence source, not the prism itself.

No replacement may silently alter admitted business meaning. Where migration affects meaning, authority, provenance or historical interpretation, that change must be explicit, versioned and testable.

## 14. First-stage work is not a limitation
Repository bootstrap, NonProd architecture, early schemas, first web pages, first adapters and early design experiments are foundations and evidence.

They may provide valuable invariants and proven skills, but they do not constrain TAGRO ECHO to first-stage technology, layout, framework, hosting mode, workflow or interaction model when later evidence supports a better solution.

Builders must preserve proven truth and obligations, not preserve accidental implementation shape.

Sunk cost is not an admission criterion.

## 15. Failure isolation
A failing component must fail calmly and contain its damage.
AI failure must not block a deterministic sale. Bank ingestion failure must not corrupt stock or service. Reporting failure must not alter operational truth. BUSY connector failure must leave an explicit reconciliation queue, not invent successful posting. A failed UI redesign must not destroy the prior recoverable candidate or corrupt business data.

## 16. Mobile and human-operating doctrine
A newly authorised employee with a phone should be able to obtain the institutional context needed to perform ordinary counter work without depending on the memory of one experienced person.
Counter Intelligence may use camera, OCR, barcode/QR, voice, text, serial recognition, shelf/workbench images, customer phone and history to accelerate work.
Every extra staff input must repay effort through faster work, better evidence, safer decisions or improved continuity.

Mobile is a first-class operating environment. User comfort, clarity, touchability, interruption recovery, visible context, reduced typing and truthful feedback are structural quality requirements.

Desktop/tablet may expose more context and analysis, but ordinary phone operation must not be treated as a degraded afterthought.

## 17. Operational source of truth
TAGRO ECHO OS is the live operational source of truth for the ECHO venture.
BUSY is a docked accounting, finance and MIS engine under separate confirmation/reconciliation rules, not the centre of live operational orchestration.
Dropbox is an independent project archive, evidence/resilience location and controlled transport surface. It is not a distributed transaction-locking mechanism.
The warehouse receives admitted data continuously and must not silently modify operational truth.

External logistics, payment, AI and other providers contribute governed results/evidence through replaceable docks; provider state does not silently replace ECHO event truth.

## 18. Time and location
Event time, observation time, source-effective time, ingestion time, confirmation time, posting time and correction time must not be collapsed when their distinction matters.
Counter, warehouse, transit, service bench, customer site, district and other material locations are first-class context.

## 19. Consequential actions
Consequential actions require authenticated actor/system identity, server-side authority, idempotency, deterministic validation, appropriate evidence/provenance, acknowledgement/result state, auditability and explicit failure/retry behaviour.

The user interface must make the consequential boundary understandable and must not imply completion merely because a command was queued or a provider UI opened.

## 20. Builder control
Every AI/human build session must begin by reading:
1. this Constitution;
2. Foundation Manifest;
3. Current State;
4. active Decision Ledger;
5. contracts for components to be touched;
6. the active Work Order.

The builder may not silently fix an out-of-scope issue. It may record an observation for later review, or seek/receive explicit owner authority to expand or supersede scope.

When persistent flaws or a credible better solution are discovered, the builder must not suppress that evidence merely because the existing work order assumed the older approach. The builder records the issue and performs or requests the Planar/Prismatic due-diligence path required by Section 13.

Every session must end with tests actually run and results, files/resources actually changed, unresolved defects, Current State update when warranted, Session Ledger entry and no completion claim beyond evidence.

## 21. Admission lifecycle
idea -> experiment -> candidate -> tested -> admitted -> active -> superseded/retired

Code existence, visual attractiveness, deployment, sunk cost or AI confidence does not equal admission.

A materially better replacement may enter through the same lifecycle without needing to imitate the component it supersedes, provided migration and truth obligations are satisfied.

## 22. User-experience structural rule
The active UI/UX contracts are structural design authority for all user-facing ECHO projections.

They govern shell, context, navigation, interaction hierarchy, touch/input behaviour, interruption/recovery, feedback, responsive/adaptive behaviour, role projection, visual comfort and accessibility.

Purpose-specific workflows may use different geometries. Consistency means coherent behaviour and language, not forcing every business job into one visual template.

The design target is to hide system complexity without hiding business truth.

## 23. Standards orientation
Engineering shall be aligned, where relevant, to current recognised standards including information security, privacy, software/product quality, business continuity, accessibility, human-computer interaction and AWS Well-Architected practice.
Alignment is not a certification claim.

Current recognised research, standards and proven shipped-product patterns may be used as evidence. They do not override ECHO reality/authority, but ECHO must not ignore materially better established practice without reason.

## 24. Constitutional change
Ordinary feature work may not modify this Constitution.
A constitutional change requires explicit owner approval, a recorded decision explaining the need, compatibility/migration impact, review of affected contracts, version increment and rollback/recovery plan.

Version 1.1 was explicitly directed by the Owner on 2026-08-22 to establish UI/UX as structural and to create a better-solution evolution rule.

Compatibility/migration impact: existing admitted events, evidence, identities, authorities and runtime truths retain their meaning. Existing first-stage implementations become candidates/evidence subject to the revised structural and evolution rules; no business record is reinterpreted by this constitutional change.

Rollback/recovery: Version 1.0 remains available in repository history. Reverting the governance text does not require data migration because Version 1.1 changes design/evolution authority rather than existing admitted event semantics. Any implementation produced under Version 1.1 still requires its own migration/rollback proof before admission.

## 25. Founding and evolution test
When uncertain, ask:

1. Does this make TAGRO ECHO more faithful to reality?
2. Is it easier and more comfortable for the real person performing the job?
3. Is it safer, more truthful and more recoverable?
4. Is it more replaceable and less dependent on hidden assumptions?
5. Does it remain suitable for TAGRO's future rather than merely preserve today's implementation?
6. Have relevant alternatives been considered across the full Planar/Prismatic field rather than through one narrow subsystem view?

If these questions cannot be demonstrated, the change remains outside the active OS or stays at candidate status until sufficient evidence exists.
